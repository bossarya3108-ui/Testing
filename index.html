Our story

A chair for every story

The Shear Room started with one belief: everyone deserves a stylist who listens. Our unisex team is trained across textures, lengths and styles — from sharp fades to long-layered transformations.

No awkward small talk unless you want it. No "men's cut / women's cut" pricing. Just honest consultation, skilled hands and a space where you can fully relax.
